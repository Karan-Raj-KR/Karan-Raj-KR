### 👋 Who I am

Bengaluru. Founder at **[KĀRYO](https://linktr.ee/Karyo)**, a digital agency where I own **backend, business development, and client outreach**. B.Tech CSE (AI/ML) at NIAT–S-VYASA.

I build AI and backend systems that go into production, not into a slide. A Chrome extension that's live on the Web Store. A multi-agent system running my own agency's sales pipeline. A voice health assistant built solo in 24 hours. I ship fast, in public, and usually under a deadline.

- 🔧 **Open source** — Project Admin on `crewai-recipes`; 18 merged PRs into GSSoC 2026
